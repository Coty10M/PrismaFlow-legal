Optional in-app purchases

Products displayed in the shop can include packs of four Destroy, Move or Refresh boosters; a Rainbow Pack with four of each type; Remove Ads; and a Starter Pack combining Remove Ads with four of each booster. Availability, local currency and final pricing are determined by the App Store at the point of purchase.

Remove Ads and the ad-removal portion of Starter Pack remove forced/interstitial advertising; optional rewarded videos may still be offered if available and you choose to use them. Booster quantities are consumable and may be depleted during play. Consumable balances are stored locally and are not guaranteed to be restored following reinstall or device change.

Purchase and reward details

Remove Ads and Starter Pack are one-time purchases, not subscriptions. The Starter Pack includes permanent ad removal and a one-time delivery of four boosters of each type; restoring the permanent entitlement does not grant the consumables again. Booster packs are consumable purchases. Optional rewarded ads grant a reward only after confirmation by the advertising provider; availability is not guaranteed. Private iCloud backups, when available, may recover a saved balance after a confirmed restore but are not an additional purchase entitlement or a guarantee of recovery.

Payments, restoration and refunds

Apple processes transactions. Use the game’s Restore Purchases feature to recheck eligible permanent purchases. Some consumables and their used balances cannot be restored. For refunds or purchase disputes, contact Apple through Report a Problem; statutory consumer rights are unaffected.
