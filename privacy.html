Privacy Policy

How your game information is stored and how optional services handle data.

Last updated: 10 November 2026
Publisher and privacy contact: Marco Cotugno · coticapp@gmail.com

Who we are

PrismaFlow is an iOS puzzle game published by Marco Cotugno (Italy). For privacy questions or requests, contact coticapp@gmail.com. This notice explains how information is handled in the app, including optional services provided by Apple and advertising partners.

Information stored on your device

To run the game, PrismaFlow saves information in local app storage on your device: unlocked levels, scores and challenge results, booster balances, your chosen nickname and preset avatar, game settings, chapter celebrations, advertising preference, and records needed to avoid delivering the same purchase or reward twice. It may also keep an identifier associated with the Game Center account used on the device to avoid mixing players’ results.

We do not operate a PrismaFlow account system or an independent online server that receives this local game profile. Apple device backups depend on your settings.

iCloud backups

If iCloud Drive is available, PrismaFlow automatically creates private backup files in your Apple iCloud account. These may include campaign progress, level records, nickname, avatar, challenge results, booster balances, settings, purchase-delivery identifiers and a device-specific backup identifier. Advertising consent, ad identifiers and Game Center account identifiers are excluded. Backups do not synchronise active games across devices; restoring a backup requires your confirmation. You can manage or delete iCloud data through Apple settings. Backup availability depends on iCloud and device settings.

Apple Game Center

If you use Game Center, Apple authenticates the player and processes Game Center information. The game submits scores, reached-level information, and daily/weekly challenge results to configured Apple leaderboards, and displays public player names, rankings and (where provided by Apple) player images. Your local nickname and selected game avatar are stored on the device and may be included in iCloud backups; Game Center uses the Apple player identity shown by Game Center. Game Center is optional.

See Apple’s Privacy Policy.

In-app purchases

Optional purchases are processed through Apple StoreKit / the App Store. We do not receive your payment card details. The app records Apple transaction identifiers and the related entitlements or booster delivery status locally, to deliver purchases and avoid duplicates. Permanent ad-removal entitlements can be checked or restored through Apple; consumable booster balances are device-local and are not guaranteed to transfer to a different device or return after reinstalling the app.

Apple handles payments, purchase records and refund requests under its own terms and privacy rules.

Advertising, consent and tracking

PrismaFlow uses Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) to display privacy choices and store or communicate consent and opt-out signals to advertising services. It uses Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK), Unity LevelPlay, Unity Ads and ironSource Ads to deliver interstitial and optional rewarded advertisements. The consent message identifies advertising partners; other authorised ad buyers and service providers may participate in ad delivery.

Advertising providers may process IP addresses and approximate location derived from them, device and app information (such as OS, model, app version and identifiers including IDFA where permitted), consent signals, ad requests, impressions, interactions, diagnostic and fraud-prevention data, and attribution or measurement information. Purposes include ad delivery, security, fraud prevention, measurement and, when permitted, personalization. Providers’ data practices and retention are described in their own notices.

Where European consent rules apply, the app uses the choices you make in UMP to select its advertising path. If consent is declined, it may request Google Limited Ads rather than initialize LevelPlay without the required device-access consent. Limited Ads do not use personal data for ad personalization; IP addresses and technical delivery data may still be processed. When programmatic limited ads are enabled, Google may use local storage or cookies solely for invalid-traffic and fraud prevention, even without advertising consent. Ads may be unavailable. See Google’s Limited Ads information.

Apple App Tracking Transparency (ATT) is separate from advertising consent. If you deny ATT, the app must not track you across other companies’ apps or websites for advertising or measurement using Apple’s tracking permission; this does not necessarily prevent advertising. Privacy choices are available in Settings → Advertising privacy when required for your region. In applicable US states, the privacy message allows opt-outs of sale, sharing or targeted advertising as applicable. Remove Ads removes forced interstitials, not optional rewarded ads.

Learn more: Google Privacy Policy, How Google uses information from partner apps, Unity Privacy Policy (including Unity advertising and ironSource services), and Apple Privacy Policy.

Why and on what basis information is handled

We process gameplay and backup records to provide the requested game and recovery features; purchase records to fulfil transactions and legal obligations; and support messages to respond to requests. We rely on performance of the requested service, legal obligations, or legitimate interests in operating, securing and supporting the game, as applicable. Advertising uses consent where required, including for personalization and non-essential storage or access to the device. Other processing, such as technical ad delivery or fraud prevention, may rely on legitimate interests where permitted; this does not override any separate consent requirement for device access. You can withdraw consent or object to legitimate-interest processing where applicable.

Apple and advertising providers receive data as needed for their services and may process it under their own privacy policies. Data may be processed outside your country, including in the US; applicable data protection safeguards and transfer mechanisms depend on the provider and processing. We do not sell your local game save or run a proprietary player-account database.

Storage, retention and security

Local game information generally stays on your device until you reset or remove it, subject to iOS backups and device settings. We do not specify retention periods for records independently maintained by Apple or advertising providers; their own privacy terms apply. Support emails are retained only as reasonably needed to respond, keep a record of the request and meet any legal obligations. We use the protections offered by iOS for locally stored app data.

Your choices and rights

Manage consent or opt-out choices in Settings → Advertising privacy when the form is available for your region; withdraw consent without affecting prior lawful processing. Manage ATT in iOS Settings, Game Center in Apple settings, and local and iCloud backup data through device and Apple account settings. Uninstalling the app removes local data but does not necessarily delete iCloud backups or provider records. For access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability, objections or applicable US privacy rights, contact coticapp@gmail.com. We may need to verify your request.

You can complain to your local supervisory authority, including the Italian data protection authority. Apple and advertising providers may need to handle requests relating to data they independently control.

Children and families

PrismaFlow is a puzzle game. We do not knowingly request children’s contact details through an in-game registration form. Children should use the app with parental supervision where appropriate. Parents or guardians can manage Apple purchase, Game Center and device privacy controls and contact us about a child’s data. Applicable age-related privacy rules and advertising restrictions must be respected; the app’s age rating does not by itself establish eligibility for personalized ads.

This website and changes

These public legal pages contain no analytics code or author-installed tracking cookies. GitHub Pages, as hosting provider, may process technical connection logs under its own privacy terms. We may update this notice when app features, partners or legal requirements change; the date at the top reflects the latest revision. Please revisit it after major releases.
