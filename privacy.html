Privacy Policy

How your game information is stored and how optional services handle data.

Last updated: 7 November 2026
Publisher and privacy contact: Marco Cotugno · cotyapp@gmail.com

Who we are

PrismaFlow is an iOS puzzle game published by Marco Cotugno (Italy). For privacy questions or requests, contact cotyapp@gmail.com. This notice explains how information is handled in the app, including optional services provided by Apple and advertising partners.

Information stored on your device

To run the game, PrismaFlow saves information in local app storage on your device: unlocked levels, scores and challenge results, booster balances, your chosen nickname and preset avatar, game settings, chapter celebrations, advertising preference, and records needed to avoid delivering the same purchase or reward twice. It may also keep an identifier associated with the Game Center account used on the device to avoid mixing players’ results.

We do not operate a PrismaFlow account system or an independent online server that receives this local game profile. Device and Apple backup behavior is controlled by your device settings.

Apple Game Center

If you use Game Center, Apple authenticates the player and processes Game Center information. The game submits scores, reached-level information, and daily/weekly challenge results to configured Apple leaderboards, and displays public player names, rankings and (where provided by Apple) player images. Your local nickname and selected game avatar are stored on the device; Game Center uses the Apple player identity shown by Game Center. Game Center is optional.

See Apple’s Privacy Policy.

In-app purchases

Optional purchases are processed through Apple StoreKit / the App Store. We do not receive your payment card details. The app records Apple transaction identifiers and the related entitlements or booster delivery status locally, to deliver purchases and avoid duplicates. Permanent ad-removal entitlements can be checked or restored through Apple; consumable booster balances are device-local and are not guaranteed to transfer to a different device or return after reinstalling the app.

Apple handles payments, purchase records and refund requests under its own terms and privacy rules.

Advertising and tracking

The app contains an integration for Unity LevelPlay to support interstitial advertisements and optional rewarded videos. At the date of this notice, production advertising is disabled in the release configuration and the in-app advertising preference screen is still a development/test implementation. The production ad configuration and consent management must be completed before ads are enabled.

If advertising is activated in a later version, Unity LevelPlay and the advertising networks configured in the mediation platform may process technical device information, IP-derived approximate location, ad identifiers where permitted, and ad delivery, fraud-prevention and measurement information, subject to their settings and applicable permissions. The actual partner list and data disclosures must be checked and this notice updated before activation. Apple’s App Tracking Transparency permission is separate from any other consent required for advertising technologies.

Relevant information: Unity Privacy Policy and Apple Privacy Policy.

Why and on what basis information is handled

Local gameplay records are processed to provide the game and remember your choices (performance of the requested service and, where applicable, legitimate interest in maintaining app functionality). Purchases are handled to fulfil the transaction and comply with applicable obligations. Optional Game Center features are provided when you use them. If advertising services are enabled, any required consent or other lawful basis must be established for the specific technologies and purposes before use.

Storage, retention and security

Local game information generally stays on your device until you reset or remove it, subject to iOS backups and device settings. We do not specify retention periods for records independently maintained by Apple or advertising providers; their own privacy terms apply. Support emails are retained only as reasonably needed to respond, keep a record of the request and meet any legal obligations. We use the protections offered by iOS for locally stored app data.

Your choices and rights

You can choose not to use Game Center, manage Apple tracking permission in iOS Settings, and contact us about privacy matters at cotyapp@gmail.com. In the current testing build you can change the advertising preference from the app settings; it is not a substitute for a completed production consent management solution. To stop the app’s local processing, you can remove the app and its local data, subject to your backup settings.

Where applicable, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability, or object to certain processing; you may withdraw consent when processing relies on it. You may also complain to your data-protection authority (in Italy, Garante per la protezione dei dati personali). Requests concerning data controlled directly by Apple or other providers may also need to be directed to those providers.

Children and families

PrismaFlow is a puzzle game. Families should supervise children’s use of Game Center, purchases and advertising functions as appropriate. We do not knowingly request children’s contact details through an in-game registration form. Before advertising is enabled, age-related obligations and the ad networks’ child-directed settings must be reviewed for the app’s final age rating and audience.

This website and changes

These public legal pages contain no analytics code or author-installed tracking cookies. GitLab Pages, as hosting provider, may process technical connection logs under its own privacy terms. We may update this notice when app features, partners or legal requirements change; the date at the top reflects the latest revision. Please revisit it after major releases.
