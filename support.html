Support

Help with the game, purchases, rewarded ads and leaderboards.

Last updated: 10 November 2026
Publisher and privacy contact: Marco Cotugno · coticapp@gmail.com

Contact us

For assistance, email coticapp@gmail.com. Please include your device model, iOS version, PrismaFlow app version, the chapter or level involved, and the steps to reproduce the issue. Screenshots are helpful. Never send passwords, payment card numbers or Apple verification codes.

Restore purchases

In the game, open Shop → Restore Purchases to check your permanent ad-removal entitlement. You must be using the appropriate Apple account. Consumable boosters are stored locally and are not guaranteed to return after changing or restoring devices. If a purchase is missing, provide Apple’s purchase date and product name in your message, but not any full payment details.

Refunds and billing

Purchases are handled by Apple. For refund requests, mistaken purchases and billing issues, use Apple Report a Problem.

Ads and rewarded videos

Rewarded videos grant a booster or another in-game benefit only after the advertising partner confirms the completed viewing. Advertising availability depends on your privacy choices, network connectivity and ad supply. Manage applicable consent choices in Settings → Advertising privacy. Remove Ads disables forced/interstitial advertisements, not necessarily optional rewarded videos.

Game Center and rankings

To use worldwide rankings, check that Game Center is enabled and you are signed in to Apple Game Center in iOS Settings. If scores do not appear immediately, check connectivity and retry later. Four leaderboard types are planned: worldwide score, reached level, daily challenge and weekly survival.

Game progress and special crystals

Progress and booster balances are saved on the device. Backups and reinstallations may affect what can be recovered. In chapter-based gameplay, Fire and Ice crystals follow their own in-game tutorial and charge/activation rules. If an activation appears wrong, send a screenshot and the level number.

Privacy requests

For access, correction, deletion or questions about locally held data and third-party services, email coticapp@gmail.com and read the Privacy Policy.
